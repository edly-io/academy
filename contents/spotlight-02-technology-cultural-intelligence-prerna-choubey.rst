What role does cultural intelligence (CQ) play in preparing learners for a globalized, tech-driven world—and how can immersive technology help teach it?

In this thoughtful conversation, learning strategist **Prerna Chaubey** unpacks the intersection of **CQ, curriculum design, and immersive learning tools like AI and VR**. Drawing on real-world examples and grounded research, she explores how technology can become a bridge—not a barrier—to empathy and understanding in the classroom.

The discussion covers:

* What cultural intelligence is, and why it matters in education
* How to meaningfully teach CQ in schools and universities
* The challenges of bias and personalization in AI-driven learning
* The evolving role of VR in delivering inclusive and immersive learning experiences
* Practical ways to embed CQ into curriculum and tech design

This interview is a valuable resource for educators, instructional designers, and technologists aiming for responsible innovation in education.
